          01 // about
          
Academic Leader & Security Researcher

        


        

          

            

            

              Md. Hasanuzzaman serves as the Head of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
              at the Canadian University of Bangladesh (CUB), where he leads academic programs, curriculum development and
              faculty initiatives that prepare students for the rapidly evolving technology landscape.
            

            

              Educated in both Bangladesh and Canada, he earned his B.Sc. from Khulna University and his
              M.Sc. in Information Technology from the University of Waterloo, Canada. He is currently pursuing his
              Ph.D., deepening his work at the intersection of networking and security.
            

            

              His research focuses on Computer Networking, Cyber Security, Network Security and Data Security,
              with published work spanning mobile IoT architectures for smart cities and intelligent energy systems.
